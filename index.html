06 / Good to know

Your
questions.

Everything you need to know before preparing an enquiry.

Is this a physical salon?

The public listing describes House of Hair as a mobile shop selling hair extensions. A walk-in salon location or salon appointments have not been verified.

Are these the exact products available?

No. The images are style references. The actual range, prices, colours and stock must be confirmed by House of Hair.

Do you deliver in Harare?

The business advertises free deliveries in the CBD and Avenues in its public listing. Ask about current coverage and terms.

Can I select a particular length or colour?

Yes. The enquiry form lets you specify your preferences. This does not confirm product availability.

Can I order or pay on this website?

No. This is an independent design benchmark. You can test the enquiry flow and copy the sample message, but no real orders or payments are processed.
